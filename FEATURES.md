# Features

- Sample sign-in and sign-out, with no credential fields.
- Plan a fictional research run, filter and sort 220 sample results, page through the feed, and reopen saved runs.
- Browse five sample Monitor issues, their charts, source summaries and collector cards.
- Compare Runs & Costs in IDR and USD and open individual run ledgers.
- Read 13 report pages each in English and Indonesian.
- Review the sample Users directory, news and organisation Settings, and demo Profile.

Collector controls, report generation and export, and account and organisation editing are illustrative.
