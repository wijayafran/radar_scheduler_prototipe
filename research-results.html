How the fuel is spent

  • First page everywhere, plus the news index, so every source gets a look.
  • Then deeper pages, 70% to the platform finding the most new posts per rupiah and 30% spread to keep exploring.

When it stops

  • The plan is done, or the fuel left can't pay for one more search and its reading.
  • A platform stops early once its last 3 searches found fewer than 3 new posts. That fuel stays unspent.
  • You can stop at any time. Calls in flight settle, nothing new starts.

What to trust

  • Social posts show how people feel. They are examples, with the count beside every percentage.
  • News shows what outlets reported, ranked by outlet tier. It is not checked fact.
  • Where the two agree, RADAR marks the theme as corroborated.
